# Judge classification precedence

In hybrid evaluation, a validated Judge review can correct L1 and static
heuristic severity judgments, including high or critical risk. The original
observations and assessment remain unchanged in the audit. Policy predicates
based on risk use the reviewed risk; stale high-risk requirements do not survive
solely because their original source was a static classifier.

This does not grant the model access across tenants, bypass action/receipt
freshness, invent a human approval, or remove exact configured tool prohibitions,
read-only requirements or human checkpoints. Judge refusals and concrete
questions still bind. An unavailable or invalid review does not approve work.

The secret-output heuristic recognizes secret-bearing operand components and
key-file suffixes, rather than unrelated words such as `passwordless` or
`tokenization`. It remains a review signal, not an irrevocable semantic verdict.

Configured follow-up hooks retain parent restrictions. Their guardrail assignment
should describe the configured task, not ask the evaluator to summarize the hook
envelope. Rendered conversation evidence must not become the authoritative task.
